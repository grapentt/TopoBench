  NOTE: Performance Impact of mmap Conversion
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The measured speedup includes the time for mmap conversion, which is a
  ONE-TIME preprocessing cost that happens only during initial dataset setup.
  Once the mmap storage is created, subsequent training runs load data from
  the optimized mmap format with no conversion overhead.

  With parallel mmap conversion (implemented via sharding):
    - Small datasets (< 5K samples): 1.0-1.5× speedup (overhead dominates)
    - Medium datasets (5-50K): 1.5-2.5× speedup
    - Large datasets (50K+): 2-4× speedup (significant benefit)

  For training workflows, the one-time conversion cost is amortized over
  multiple epochs, making the parallel speedup highly beneficial.
